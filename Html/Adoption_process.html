Step-by-Step Adoption Process

Our structured 7-stage pathway guarantees pet-to-owner compatibility, safety, and a seamless transition from shelter to your home.

S1Step 01

Browse Available Pets

Explore profiles of rescue animals. Filter by age, breed, temperament, energy level, and special medical needs.

S2Step 02

Submit Application

Fill in our intuitive digital questionnaire covering household dynamics, yard setup, schedule, and previous pet history.

S3Step 03

Screening & Review

Our placement counselors assess application details within 24–48 hours to confirm optimal match and lifestyle suitability.

S4Step 04

Meet & Greet Online

Participate in an interactive video session or scheduled in-person visit with the pet, foster parent, and shelter coordinator.

S5Step 05

Living Conditions Docs

Upload proof of address, landlord pet policy approvals, and short video or photos of the dedicated living/yard area.

S6Step 06

Approval & Payment

Receive your formal adoption endorsement and safely process the subsidized adoption fee via our encrypted portal.

S7Final Step

Welcome Home (Pickup)

Schedule the official homecoming! Collect health dossiers, microchip tags, initial starter transition supplies, and celebrate welcoming your newest companion to their forever home.

Requirements & Eligibility

Please ensure you satisfy these foundational qualifications prior to submitting your formal adoption application.

01

Age & Identification

Applicants must be at least 18 years of age (21 for select large breeds) with a valid government-issued photo ID (driver's license, passport, or national ID card).

Mandatory Identification
02

Housing & Landlord Approval

Proof of homeownership or direct written consent/lease addendum from your landlord confirming pet allowances, weight restrictions, and breed regulations.

Property Verification
03

Current Pet & Vacc Records

Existing pets in the household must be spayed/neutered and up-to-date on essential rabies, DHPP, and core vaccinations, corroborated by veterinarian records.

Veterinary Reference
04

Ongoing Care Commitment

Demonstrated financial and emotional readiness to provide high-quality food, annual wellness checkups, emergency care, daily exercise, and lifelong commitment.

Lifelong Guardianship

HAVE QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do the application processing and review timelines take?

Standard initial reviews take between 24 to 48 hours. Once your references, housing documents and veterinary checkups are confirmed, we coordinate the virtual Meet & Greet within 2-3 business days. Most adoptions conclude in under 7 business days.

What are your apartment & Landlord Policies? Do I need a fenced yard?

A fenced yard is not strictly required for all dogs. We evaluate each application based on the specific needs of the animal and your commitment to leash walking and exercise. Written landlord approval is mandatory for renters.

What are your trial & Return Policies if the match doesn't work out?

We offer a 2-week adjustment period with full support from our behavioral team. If the match is ultimately unsuccessful, we require the pet to be returned to Adoptly to ensure their continued safety.

Can I adopt an animal if I reside outside the local state or region?

Currently, we facilitate adoptions within a 150-mile radius of our main headquarters to ensure we can provide adequate home checks and follow-up support.
